Tarteel Essence

وَرَتِّلِ الْقُرْآنَ تَرْتِيلًا

"And recite the Qur'an in slow measured rhythmic tone" (73:4)

Tarteel means reciting slowly and making letters clear. This assists in understanding and pondering the divine message.
