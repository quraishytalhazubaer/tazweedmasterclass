Lesson 09: Clarity & Change

Iqlaab & Izhaar

Iqlaab (Conversion)

إقلاب

مِنْ بَعْدِ

إظهار

Lesson 10: Concealment

Ikhfa (Hiding)

Definition

Ikhfa means 'concealment or hiding'.
When the Noon Saakin or Tanween is followed by any of the Ikhfa letters, the sound should be suppressed and pronounced with Ghunnah.

إخفاء
