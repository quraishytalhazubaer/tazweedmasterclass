The Essence of Recitation

وَرَتِّلِ الْقُرْآنَ تَرْتِيلًا

"And recite the Qur'an in slow measured rhythmic tone" (73:4)

Tarteel means to recite slowly, making the letters clear. This assists in understanding and pondering the divine message. As Ibn Kathir stated, it is the key to spiritual connection with the text.

Basic Movements (Harakat)

◌َ

Fattah (Zabar)

The "A" sound. A diagonal stroke above the letter.

◌ِ

Kasrah (Zer)

The "I" sound. A diagonal stroke below the letter.

◌ُ

Dammah (Pesh)

The "U" sound. A small loop above the letter.

◌ْ

Sakin (Jazam)

Absence of a vowel. Connects the letter to the one before it.

3 Alifs

Madd-e-Ardee

نَسْتَعِينُ
3 Alifs

Madd-e-Munfasil

Separated Madd across two words.

بِمَا أُنْزِلَ

Madd-e-Lazim & Muttasil

4 Alifs

Madd-e-Muttasil (Joined)

Madd letter and Hamzah in the same word.

جَاءَ , السَّمَاء

Must be prolonged for 4 counts.

4-5 Alifs

Madd-e-Lazim (Compulsory)

Followed by Sakin or Tasdeed.

الضَّالِّينَ , آلْآنَ

Found in Huroof-e-Muqatta'at and words with Shaddah.
